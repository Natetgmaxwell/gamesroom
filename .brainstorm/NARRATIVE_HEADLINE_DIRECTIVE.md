## Why this combination

A is the **product claim** (the app is invisible during play). C is the **cultural claim** (we are against the trend you are exhausted by). Paired, they land as "this is the product AND this is why your life is better for it" — two opens in one story, no feed on either.

The Mirror Application Test: a non-tech-curious adult hears pairing 1 and learns what Games Room does (option A) — this is the platform-level claim. Pairing 2 leads with *anti-trend* — for an audience that already knows the platform-trend is exhausting. Pairing 1 is the default for the website hero because it teaches; Pairing 2 is the default for social posts because it does NOT teach — the canvas is too small to teach anything.
