## One canon correction (must propagate to gbrain)

- **The 50 / 90 / 200-hour rule is Jeffrey Hall (Univ. Kansas, *Journal of Social and Personal Relationships*, 2019). It is NOT Waldinger.**
- Waldinger / Schulz (Harvard Study of Adult Development) claim is the **age-50 relationship-quality is the strongest predictor of long-term health and happiness**. Waldinger's book is *The Good Life* (2023).
- Hall's rule is the time-to-acquaintance → casual friend → close friend curve.
- Both are real; they are different. Fix any brain page that credits Waldinger with the hours curve.

## Top design patterns, ranked by Games Room applicability

### Tier 1 — load-bearing
1. **Recurring-slot ritual** over event-by-event discovery (Timeleft) → default Weekly Default per room.
2. **Host is the leader, app is the surface** (Meetup, Luma) → already true in Games Room.
3. **Ledger as social surface** (BGG, MTG, chips-withdrawn) → already true, deepen with story.
4. **Anti-flake as product, not friction** (deposit-forfeit already exists) → make the forfeit visible (no-show tax into next pot).
5. **Friends-of-friends as bridge** (Soho House, NFX) → invite tier 2 = member + co-host approval.

### Tier 2 — strong leverage
6. Quiet-by-default communications. No in-app chat. SMS for urgent. Push only for confirm-by-deadline.
7. The venue is the room, the app is the front door. Vision-settle as the *front door to the ledger* (Pontis framing).
8. Multi-room per user, ledger-merged across rooms (Strava's personal-stats unification).
9. The room as arena, the app as medium (MTG stores, BGG).
10. Friction is the signal. Low-friction invites attract flakes; the deposit IS the friction; add "confirm by Tuesday at 9pm."

### Tier 3 — leverage if time allows
11. Hosts curate room identity (room profile page, signature game, house rules).
12. Season arc + leaderboard. Finite window = shared end-state (fantasy football, Commander leagues).

## 12 concrete Games Room moves (product bets with attribution)

1. **The Weekly Default** — every room ships with a recurring slot the host can move but doesn't have to schedule. *Timeleft; Haidt inoculation.*
2. **No-show Tax** — forfeited deposits surface as a visible, room-wide "no-show tax" added to next pot. *Currier shared hardship; BGG play-log publicness.*
3. **Ledger-as-Story** — weekly "this week in [room]" recap: top mover, longest streak, biggest chip swing. *BGG BG Stats; Luma host profile; Aristotle pleasure-friends.*
4. **The Public Yes-List** — RSVP is public, on the iPad dashboard, not a private DM. *Putnam visibility; Soho House curation; friction-is-signal.*
5. **Friends-of-Friends Bridge** — T2 invites require one co-host approval. *Soho House vetting; Meetup group sovereignty.*
6. **Cross-Room Stats** — unified stats across rooms. *Strava; Currier high overlap.*
7. **Season Arc** — every room runs a season. 8 weeks. 12 weeks. "Ends at the playoff." *Fantasy football; WPN Commander leagues; Aristotle utility → virtue.*
8. **Quiet-by-Default** — no in-app chat. The room's life is the ledger + calendar. *Oldenburg; Luma minimum-surface; Partiful boop-emoji.*
9. **House Rule / Signature Game** — each room has a "core format." Host picks it. Shows in room profile. *Commander boutique format; Soho House brands.*
10. **The Reveal** — vision-first settle as the front door to the ledger. *Pontis "front door"; Luma event-as-surface.*
11. **The Stranger-Maker** — once per season, a T2 invite actually gets *everyone* in the room = deliberate friendship injection. *Aristotle introduction; Putnam bridging capital.*
12. **Health-Floor Pulse** — track consecutive weeks the room has run + members each member has seen at least 3 times. Host-dashboard vital signs. *Surgeon General; Waldinger/Schulz age-50; social fitness.*

## What NOT to build (the single risk)

**Become the medium.**

Every case study that succeeded here — Meetup, Luma, Timeleft, MTG, Strava, Soho House — shares one move: **the platform is the front door to the room, not the room.** Every case study that died — IRL, Yik Yak, Path — shares the opposite move: the platform was the medium, and the medium got either hollow or mean.

The temptation will be: more feed, more social graph, more notifications, more engagement. **Refuse all of it.** Every metric Games Room cares about should be a metric that *only fires when a night actually happened*. A forfeit is a real event. A friend-of-friend who actually showed up is a real event. A chip swing across three rooms is a real event. Everything else is noise.

Secondary risks worth flagging briefly:
- **No feed.** Once a post is public, the room becomes a content object.
- **No DMs.** DM features turn the app into a group-chat app. The room IS the chat.
- **No invite-link virality.** Friends-of-friends is a principled boundary; virality is unprincipled. The People Network (NFX) and Soho House both depend on bounded density for trust.

Thesis: *Games Room is the counter-trend to social media. Tech facilitates social interactions, doesn't replace them. The app plans nights, doesn't run them.* The discipline is to keep the app below the table.
