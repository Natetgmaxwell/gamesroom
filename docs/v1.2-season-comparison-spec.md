## The spec (locked, one commit per level)

**Level 0 — no tap (settled/room page "Seasons" section):**
Each season = one horizontal bar, length proportional to YOUR final score that season. Current season's bar = progress so far, with a ghost tick marking where last season ENDED — "am I beating last season?" answered by bar-vs-tick with zero reading. Gain/loss via existing Palette.gain/.loss only where they carry data; past seasons' bars in grey/muted; your row aligned with the standings row treatment. Section title stays "Past seasons" or becomes "Seasons" — pick what reads naturally; no number is REMOVED (numbers shrink to secondary or move one tap down, they don't vanish).

**Level 1 — tap a season row:**
The dual trace: your cumulative points night-by-night this season (accent line) overlaid on the same-nights trace of the previous season (grey ghost). The gap between the lines is the story. One plain sentence beneath, e.g. "Ahead by 240 with two nights left." No axes, no gridlines, no legend — at most a first/last point label, muted.

**Level 2 — the season grid (Land-or-Water):**
One row per game night, one column per member, cell colour intensity = points won that night (green scale, red for negative). You should RECOGNISE the season's shape: who dominates, who's streaky, the night it flipped. Tap a cell → the verbatim numbers. No axes/legends; column headers = member initials/monograms (existing pattern), row markers = relative dates ("3w ago").
