# V1.2 Slice 2b — Settled View Story + Aesthetic Simplification
Locked by Nathan 2026-10-14 ("lock 2b").

Nathan's direction (verbatim, 2026-10-14): "I still think we can do better. we need to simplify the aethetic. make the design more human facing. also the settled view needs some works for human readibility and improved layout."

Locked spec (presented to Nathan and approved as-is):
1. **Hero card says the story in plain type.** The settled hero ("Borat held the table." / "Settled 30 minutes ago" / next event / Tonight's Star) drops the serif/typewriter affect; the mascot caption becomes ONE small italic line at the card's foot — personality in the margins, never the headline.
2. **Host machinery gets out of the way.** The reaction chips row + "Pick a member ⌄" + gold "Save the call" button collapse behind ONE quiet "Make a call" action (text button, trailing chevron) that opens the existing pick-member + reaction flow in a sheet. The settled view stops being a workflow form with a summary bolted on. The host flow must still fully work — moved, not deleted.
3. **Kill the typewriter quote.** The alternating-case mascot quote ("bOrAt: FrIdAy NiGhT hOlD'eM…", seen at the foot of the room page) is the biggest AI-tell on screen. Plain sentence in normal case, or drop entirely. No new aphorisms.
4. **Standings readability.** Score column right-aligned, monospaced; delta one consistent treatment (+green/−red, same size every row); "N sessions" muted, same position every row. No two-line right-side squint. (This is the text-layer base for 2c sparklines — keep the row data shape stable.)
5. **Aesthetic pass, cheap wins app-wide:** sentence case everywhere (no uppercase tracking labels), colour on data only (one accent rule), decorative glyphs ("⌐"-style) removed, icons only where they carry meaning.

Evidence base (TARS screenshot review of the settled state, 2026-10-14): hero recap keeps a rounded card with chips-inside (fine — Hearth hero — but its interior must simplify per items 1–2); standings right side is a two-line squint (score over tiny orange delta over muted sessions); the alternating-case quote sits at the page foot. Reference bar: Superhuman/Mail/Things — flat, type-hierarchy, personality in margins. Nathan's standing taste: rejects card/chip/pill chrome; hierarchy via type scale + weight + grey; no em-dashes in product copy; mascot lines must sound like a person, not a writer performing.
