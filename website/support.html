Support

Games Room is a small app, run by a small team. If something is wrong, we want to know.

Contact

Email: support@gamesroom.app

We read every message. Please include:

  • Your room name, if you have one
  • What you were doing when the problem happened
  • What you expected to happen

What we can help with

  • Joining or leaving a room
  • Invite codes
  • Scores and seasons
  • The mascot
  • Anything else that is not working

What we cannot help with

Games Room is invite-only. We cannot add you to a room, and we cannot create one for you. Ask the host of your room for an invite.

Privacy

Your data stays in your rooms. Read the full privacy policy.
