Games Room

The app for your games night.

Games Room is a private app for in-person games nights. You bring your people. The app plans the night, runs the scores, and stays quiet during play.

A host creates a room and shares a six-character invite code. Members join, claim seats, and RSVP. Every room is invite-only.

Four packs ship pre-installed

  • Casino
  • Cards Against Humanity
  • Monopoly Deal
  • Pluto Chess

Seasons give each pack a score arc

Scores carry between nights and reset when the season ends. The leaderboard tracks who actually wins across months, not just who won last Tuesday.

Every room gets a mascot

Pick its personality and politics at room-create, and it sends the reminders, narrates the night, and wraps each session in one line.

No ads. No analytics. No third-party SDKs. Sign in with Apple; we never see your email.
