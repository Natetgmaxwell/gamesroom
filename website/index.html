Why Games Room exists

Come for the games. Stay for the conversation.

What the talk sounds like

Serious, then stupid, then serious again.

A good night does not stay on one level. The talk goes up to big ideas. Then it goes down to nonsense. Then it goes up again.

Each game gives the table a new start. Nobody must find the next topic alone.

Where the phone goes

Before. Face down. After.

  1. Invites. Seats. Reminders.

  2. Phones face down.

  3. Scan. Settle. Tonight's Star.

The app does its work before the night and after the night.

During play, the phones stay face down. If a game needs a score, the host enters it.

How a night works

Five steps. Tap one to see it.

Step 1 Join a room A friend gives you a code. You type it once.

  1. The host makes a code. Each code has six characters.

  2. The host sends one code to one friend.

  3. The friend types the code and joins. After that, the code does not work.

Members can make codes too. The host sets how many each member can hold.

Step 2 The night is set The host picks a date and the games. The room gets the news.

  1. The host picks a date, a time and the games.

  2. The new night shows in the room for each member at the same time.

  3. Reminders come one week before, three to four days before, and on the morning of the night.

Reminders are off until you turn them on for a room. If you tap Can't make it, the reminders stop.

Step 3 Claim a seat One tap puts your name on a chair.

  1. Tap Claim seat. That is all.

  2. Your name goes on a chair. Everyone can see who is coming.

  3. The room can hold a seat deposit. Tap I'm here at the night and you get it back.

Plans change. Release your seat and it opens for another person.

Step 4 Play and score You play with real chips. The app counts them at the end.

  1. Take points from your balance in the app.

  2. The host gives you real chips for those points.

  3. You play. The phones stay face down.

  4. At the end, scan your stack. The ledger records what you won or lost.

Some games have one winner, like Monopoly Deal. In those games, the host taps the winner of each round.

Step 5 The season Scores carry from night to night. Then the awards go out.

  1. Scores carry from night to night.

  2. The host ends the season. The app gives the awards.

  3. Each balance goes back to the start. A new season begins.

What a season looks like

The night compounds.

  1. Week 1

    Three people claim a seat. The first night happens.

  2. Week 4

    Five seats are full. Somebody brings chips.

  3. Week 8

    The season ends. The awards go out, and the table laughs.

  4. Week 12

    A friend of a friend sits down. Nobody treats them as new.

What we will not build

Look at the table, not the screen.

  • No feed
  • No DMs
  • No follows
  • No streaks

Some features keep your eyes on the screen. We do not build them. Your eyes belong on the people at the table.

No ads. No analytics. No third-party SDKs. Sign in with Apple. We never see your email.
