Why Games Room exists

The night dies in the thread.

The host opens the group chat. "Who's in Friday?" Two replies, one maybe, then the thread scrolls on.

The night dies there: coordination is the whole job, and the thread is bad at it.

But the night was never the problem. The coordination was. Games Room takes that job off the host's hands.

Plan the night. Hold the seats. Keep the scores. Get out of the way.

The host

Three taps and the night is on. The room is set, the RSVPs are in, nobody got chased. You host because you want the night to happen, not because you want to run it.

The member

Your chair card has your name on it. You walk in, sit, and the night starts. No scanning for where you fit, no wondering if it is still on. The seat is held.
