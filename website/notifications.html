Three quiet reminders. Then silence.

Notifications are off until you turn them on for a room. When they are on, you get these messages for each night, and nothing else.

  • A message when the host makes the night.
  • One week before. The mascot tells you the night is coming.
  • Three to four days before. It reminds you again, with the host note.
  • The morning of the night, at 9 am. The night is today.
  • Off by default: Each room asks you one time. You can change it later in the room settings.
  • Stop for one night: Tap Can't make it and the reminders for that night stop. You can also mute one night.
  • Quiet during play: The app sends nothing during the night. The phone stays in your pocket.
  • No feed: There are no likes, no follows and no messages from other people. The app only tells you about the night.

"Pull up a seat. The night keeps itself."
