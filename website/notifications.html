Three quiet reminders. Then silence.

Games Room sends three notifications per night, and nothing else.

  • A push when the night is created. The room is on.
  • A 48-hour briefing. Who's coming, what's played, what to bring.
  • A morning-of reminder. The night is tonight.
  • Grouped per room: Notifications group by room on the lock screen. The poker room and the CAH room stay separate.
  • Quiet during play: During the night, the app goes quiet. The phone stays in the pocket. No pings, no feed, no livestream of the table.
  • One channel: There is one notification channel. Mute it in iOS settings and the app goes fully quiet. No in-app maze of toggles.

"Pull up a seat. The night keeps itself."
